## E-Commerce-Customer-Behavior-Analysis-EDA-Using-Python
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on an e-commerce dataset to understand customer purchasing behavior, revenue trends, and key factors influencing spending. The analysis uses Python libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

### Dataset
The dataset contains 237,000+ customer transaction records including:

### Customer demographics

Monthly income

Order amounts

Product counts

Wallet balance

City information

### Key Questions Explored
How does customer income influence spending?

Which cities generate the most revenue?

Are there differences in spending by gender?

How do product counts affect order value?

What are the monthly revenue trends?

### Key Insights
Income vs Spending
Higher income groups generally show higher spending patterns.

##### City Revenue
Delhi generates the highest revenue and has the largest concentration of high-value customers.

##### Spending Drivers
Total spending is moderately correlated with:

product order count

order amount

wallet balance

monthly income

##### Customer Segmentation
High-income customers show greater variability in spending.

##### Revenue Trends
Monthly revenue remains relatively stable between ₹120M–₹130M.
